# S1E3 thumbnail — design notes
- Text: "IT'S OPEN" (yellow #ffd400, Anton 250px, 16px black stroke, top-right) + "DAY 90" (white, 130px, under it). 4 words total; title not repeated.
- Focal moment: day 90 cliffhanger — the car-deck chain is on the floor and the door is open a crack. YOU close-up on the left (s=3.0, ~45% of frame height), fear face + sweat drop, warm rim glow (#ffd27a), body turned right toward the door.
- Threat: black gap in the car-deck door with two pairs of RED glowing eyes and grey fingers curling round the edge (partly hidden = curiosity). Red eyes are the one saturated accent besides the yellow text.
- Background: fcar stairwell, bred light, desaturated 0.6, dimmed 0.75 (brighter than S1E2 so the door shape separates from the wall); bottom fades to black from y=880 so the bottom-right timestamp corner is empty.
- Built with tools/thumb.py v2 (thumb_shots.json shot 0 = fcar + fx chainopen/ajar with wide=6, eyes, eyey, grab; shot 1 = hero on ENV.tgreen). NB: don't name a shot field "hand" — the engine uses it for handheld camera shake.
- Checked at 320x180 (work/s1e3/thumb_320.jpg): face, red eyes in the gap and both text lines readable.
